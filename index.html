About Me

Welcome to my website! I'm a student at Northeastern University in Boston, majoring in Data Science and Business Administration with a concentration in Finance. I expect to graduate in December 2026. Some of the courses I've taken include Business Statistics, Programming with Data, and Microeconomics, and I made the Dean's List in Fall 2025. What I like most about my major is that it combines two things I enjoy: working with data and figuring out how businesses make decisions.

Outside of school, I've spent a lot of time in finance and investment. I've interned at an investment management firm, a wealth management securities company, a commercial bank, and a private equity research team. Along the way I've looked at everything from fund performance to new medical technologies like brain-computer interfaces.

I also have a less typical side: construction. Since 2024 I've been the U.S. General Manager for Zhongjihua Engineering Management Group, where I research the American construction and real estate market and help manage projects. I also started a sustainability project called the Green Horizon Initiative, which promotes greener ways to build.

For my work, I mostly use Python, R, SQL, and Stata, plus finance tools like Wind and Bloomberg. I speak English and Mandarin. I made this site for my class to practice HTML, CSS, and D3, so feel free to look around. You can find more details on my Experience page.
